# Lesson 3 Plan — উত্তল লেন্স, বিবর্ধক কাচ, মানব চক্ষু ও চশমার ধারণা

Status: GATE_A_RECONCILED_PLAN
Target duration: 60 min
Planned CORE: 42 min
Planned FLEX: 8 min
Unallocated contingency: 10 min

## Learning goals

Students should be able to:

1. describe qualitatively how a convex/converging lens bends parallel rays toward a focus;
2. recognize a concave/diverging lens as spreading parallel rays apart at a qualitative level;
3. identify focus and focal length conceptually;
4. distinguish real/virtual, erect/inverted and magnified/reduced images at the level needed for this chapter;
5. explain how a magnifying glass forms a virtual, erect and enlarged image when the object is within the focal length;
6. identify major parts of the human eye and their functions;
7. explain that the cornea provides most of the eye's initial focusing and the crystalline lens fine-tunes focus through accommodation;
8. explain qualitatively how iris/pupil regulate admitted light;
9. state the basic corrective role of spectacles, clearly labelled as supplementary because the NCTB body omits this subsection.

## Lens prerequisite treatment

### Convex/converging lens
- parallel rays converge toward a focus;
- needed for magnifying-glass and eye/camera discussion.

### Concave/diverging lens
- parallel rays spread apart after passing through the lens;
- introduced only so students can understand the basic myopia-correction idea later.

No lens formula, sign convention, power calculation or numerical ray tracing in CORE.

## Human-eye structure — functional grouping

Do not teach nine labels as nine isolated mini-lectures. Group them by function:

### Protective/supporting structures
- sclera
- choroid

### Front optical/light-entry structures
- cornea
- iris
- pupil
- aqueous humour

### Focusing system
- cornea
- crystalline lens
- ciliary/accommodation idea

### Receiving/inner medium
- vitreous humour
- retina

Important scientific clarification:
- the cornea performs most of the eye's initial refractive focusing;
- the crystalline lens fine-tunes focus by changing shape/optical power during accommodation;
- avoid implying that the crystalline lens alone performs all focusing.

## Spectacles treatment

Use only a concise conceptual statement:

- spectacles place an additional lens before the eye so incoming rays reach the eye with a more useful convergence/divergence;
- myopia: diverging/concave lens reduces the system's effective converging power;
- hyperopia: converging/convex lens increases effective converging power;
- no lens-power calculation, detailed defect derivation or claim that this wording appears in the NCTB chapter body.

If classroom pacing is slow, the detailed corrective-lens diagram may move to FLEX, but the one-sentence spectacles principle remains available because the chapter opener lists the outcome.

## Major misconceptions

- pupil is a solid black object rather than an opening;
- eye lens alone provides all refractive power;
- convex lens always produces the same image type;
- concave lens has already been fully taught just because it was named — false; only qualitative action is required here;
- magnifying glass always magnifies regardless of object position;
- spectacles make the eye physically larger/smaller rather than altering ray convergence/divergence.
