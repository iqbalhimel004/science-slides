## R2-03 — Critical-angle / TIR three-state sequence

Target: L2-S08–L2-S11.

Source basis: NCTB pp.112–113; OpenStax University Physics Vol. 3, Total Internal Reflection (S4).

Required scientific states:
- `i < C`: refracted ray exists and reflected ray is also shown;
- `i = C`: refracted ray runs along the interface (`r = 90°`) **and a reflected ray remains in the first medium**;
- `i > C`: total internal reflection; no transmitted refracted ray in the idealized geometric-optics model;
- reflected ray obeys law of reflection in all three states;
- do not label/quantify reflected power fractions in this Class-8 visual.

Accessibility:
- angle labels and arrowheads visible;
- no meaning depends on color;
- three states explicitly titled.
