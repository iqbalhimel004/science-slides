## R4-04 — Film chemistry + digital contrast callout

Target: L4-S07.

Required black-and-white film sequence:
1. exposure creates latent image pattern in silver-halide material;
2. developer reduces exposed grains to metallic silver;
3. fixer/hypo removes remaining undeveloped/unexposed silver halide;
4. remaining silver pattern forms a negative.

Required banner:
`Film camera chemistry — modern digital cameras do not use this process.`

Gate-B compression:
- include one small callout only: `Digital cameras record light electronically on image-sensor pixels; they do not use developer/hypo film processing.`
- no separate CORE digital-camera panel;
- CCD/CMOS/sensor architecture is STRETCH only.

Source basis: S14/S15 for film chemistry; S19 for electronic digital recording.

## R4-05 — Eye vs camera comparison

Targets: L4-S08–L4-S10.

Shared functions:
- camera lens system ↔ cornea + crystalline lens focusing system;
- aperture/diaphragm ↔ iris/pupil light-control system;
- film/sensor receiving plane ↔ retina as receiving surface.

Required analogy limits:
- `Similar function ≠ identical structure/mechanism`;
- eyelid is not an exact optical shutter;
- retina is not a film-storage sheet;
- eye accommodates by changing crystalline-lens shape/optical power;
- conventional camera focusing may use lens/lens-group movement; do not universalize one mechanism.

Source basis: NCTB p.116 + S16/S17/S19 + SI-11-006/SI-11-007.
