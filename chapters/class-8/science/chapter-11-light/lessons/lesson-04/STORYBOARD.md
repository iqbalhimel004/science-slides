## Storyboard principles for this lesson

- build the camera from familiar lens/image concepts before part-by-part memorization;
- keep textbook film-camera chemistry clearly contextualized as film photography, not modern digital imaging;
- compare eye and camera by shared optical function, then explicitly show where the analogy breaks;
- do not imply eyelid = shutter as an exact mechanism;
- do not imply retina stores a photograph like film;
- state that the cornea + crystalline lens form the eye focusing system and that accommodation changes lens shape/optical power;
- conventional camera focusing may involve lens/lens-group movement, but no universal mechanism claim;
- finish with whole-chapter synthesis and exam-facing application;
- all CORE slides must work offline.

### L4-S06 — Shutter ও Exposure: কতক্ষণ light পৌঁছাবে?

- Segment: CORE

- Student action/question: `কোনটি সময় নিয়ন্ত্রণ করে—aperture না shutter?`

### L4-S07 — Film camera chemistry: developer → fixer → negative

- Segment: CORE
- Purpose: teach NCTB film-processing content accurately while preventing digital-camera confusion.
- Core content:
  - visible banner: `Film camera chemistry — modern digital cameras do not use this process`;
  - exposure creates a latent image pattern in silver-halide film/emulsion;
  - developer reduces exposed grains to metallic silver in the simplified black-and-white process;
  - fixer/hypo removes remaining undeveloped/unexposed silver halide;
  - remaining silver pattern forms a negative.
- Slide family/layout intent: 4-step process strip.
- Visual/resource: R4-04.
- Visual purpose: Sequence / Explain
- Teacher note: keep chemistry qualitative; do not teach developer composition or reaction equations.
- Student action/question: `Hypo/fixer-এর কাজ কী—image develop করা, নাকি undeveloped silver halide সরানো?`
- Expected response: remove undeveloped/unexposed silver halide.
- Segment-response role: hinge
- Estimated time: 5 min
- Source reference(s): NCTB p.115; C11-015; SI-11-004; S14/S15.
- Static/offline fallback: primary static process diagram.
- Transition to next slide: `এখন camera আর human eye—মিল কোথায়?`

### L4-S09 — Same function, different focusing mechanism

- Segment: CORE
- Purpose: explicitly prevent eye-camera mechanical conflation.
- Core content:
  - Eye: retina distance essentially fixed; ciliary action changes crystalline-lens shape/optical power; cornea does most initial bending;
  - Camera: focusing adjusts the optical system; in conventional cameras this commonly includes moving a lens/lens group relative to the image plane;
  - do not claim all modern cameras use the same focusing mechanism.
- Slide family/layout intent: eye two-state accommodation vs camera lens-position schematic.
- Visual/resource: R4-05 stage 2.
- Visual purpose: Compare / Explain
- Teacher note: this is a clarification slide, not a detailed camera-engineering lesson.
- Student action/question: `Eye focus করতে lens-কে retina-এর দিকে সরায়, নাকি lens-এর shape বদলায়?`
- Expected response: shape/optical power changes.
- Segment-response role: misconception refutation / hinge
- Estimated time: 3 min
- Source reference(s): S17/S19; B11-017/B11-020; SI-11-006/SI-11-007.
- Static/offline fallback: static two-panel schematic.

### L4-S10 — Analogy limits: eyelid ≠ shutter, retina ≠ film

- Segment: CORE
- Purpose: prevent over-literal textbook analogy.
- Core content:
  - eyelid can block incoming light, but it is not the camera's precise optical shutter mechanism;
  - retina receives light and initiates biological visual processing; it does not store a photographic negative like film;
  - `similar role in a comparison` ≠ `same mechanism/material`.
- Slide family/layout intent: two “similar but not identical” comparison cards.
- Visual/resource: R4-05 stage 3.
- Visual purpose: Compare / Misconception correction
- Teacher note: phrase as analogy limits, not as criticism of learning by analogy.
- Student action/question: identify one similarity and one difference.
- Expected response: both affect/receive light functionally, but mechanisms/materials differ.
- Segment-response role: explain / practice
- Estimated time: 2 min
- Source reference(s): SI-11-007; S17/S19.
- Static/offline fallback: static slide.
- Transition to next slide: `Film camera আর digital camera-র recording method-ও এক নয়।`
