### L1-S03 — আলো কী?

- Segment: CORE
- Purpose: give a scientifically current, grade-appropriate description of light.
- Core content:
  - main statement: `আলো এক ধরনের তড়িৎচৌম্বক বিকিরণ। দৃশ্যমান আলো আমাদের চোখে দৃষ্টির অনুভূতি সৃষ্টি করতে পারে।`
  - small non-assessed note: `আধুনিক কোয়ান্টাম বর্ণনায় আলোর শক্তি photon নামের discrete quanta হিসেবে আদান-প্রদান হয়।`
  - guardrail: `Photon-কে ক্ষুদ্র সাধারণ পদার্থের বলের মতো ভাববে না।`
- Slide family/layout intent: definition + one small modern-science callout.
- Visual/resource: minimal EM-wave symbol plus small photon label; avoid decorative quantum art.
- Visual purpose: Explain
- Teacher note: do not teach wave-particle duality as a separate concept; no `E=hf`.
- Student action/question: `এই photon অংশটি কি আজকের পরীক্ষার মূল বিষয়?`

- Segment-response role: explain
- Estimated time: 3 min
- Source reference(s): S11, S12.
- Static/offline fallback: static slide.
- Transition to next slide: `এই অধ্যায়ে আলোর কোন বৈশিষ্ট্যগুলো আমাদের দরকার?`

### L1-S08 — Reflection না Refraction?

- Segment: CORE
- Purpose: distinguish reflection from refraction and introduce incident/refracted ray plus `i`, `r`.
- Core content:
  - incident ray;
  - refracted ray;
  - `i = angle of incidence`;
  - `r = angle of refraction`;
  - reflection: light returns into first medium;
  - refraction: light enters second medium and may change direction.
- Slide family/layout intent: side-by-side comparison with one fresh unlabeled mini-diagram.
- Visual/resource: reflection vs refraction paired ray diagrams.
- Visual purpose: Compare / Explain / Practice
- Teacher note: emphasize `may change direction`, preserving normal-incidence exception for next slides.
- Student action/question: identify `i` and `r` on the fresh diagram; answer whether pencil-in-water is mainly refraction or reflection.
- Expected response: correct labels; refraction.
- Segment-response role: hinge / practice
- Estimated time: 4 min
- Source reference(s): NCTB printed pp.109–110 / PDF pp.114–115; S2/S3; SI-11-002.
- Static/offline fallback: static diagrams.
- Transition to next slide: `কিন্তু ray বাঁকে কেন?`

### L1-S09 — Ray বাঁকে কেন? বেগ বদলায়

- Segment: CORE
- Purpose: connect medium change to speed change and qualitative refraction.
- Core content:
  - `মাধ্যম বদলালে আলোর বেগ বদলাতে পারে।`
  - `তির্যকভাবে interface অতিক্রম করলে এই speed change-এর কারণে ray-এর direction বদলাতে পারে।`
  - optical density clarification: `Optically denser মানেই বেশি mass density নয়।`
  - qualitative relation for this lesson: `যে তুলনায় আলো ধীরে চলে, সেই medium-কে তুলনামূলকভাবে optically denser বলা হয়।`
- Slide family/layout intent: before/after medium comparison with ray path.
- Visual/resource: two media with same ray entering obliquely; speed labels conceptual, not numerical.
- Visual purpose: Explain
- Teacher note: do not add unsourced kerosene/water numbers. Avoid implying a boundary mechanically pushes light.
- Student action/question: `Optically denser মানেই কি বেশি ভারী/বেশি mass density?`
- Expected response: no.
- Segment-response role: misconception refutation
- Estimated time: 3 min
- Source reference(s): S2, S3, S13; SI-11-002.
- Static/offline fallback: static diagram.
- Transition to next slide: `তবে medium বদলালেই কি ray সবসময় বাঁকবে?`

### L1-S10 — Normal incidence: বেগ বদলালেও দিক বদলায় না

- Slide family/layout intent: side-by-side contrast.

- Expected response: the normal ray; incidence is along the normal.
- Segment-response role: hinge / predict
- Estimated time: 3 min
- Source reference(s): NCTB printed pp.110–111 / PDF pp.115–116; S2/S3; SI-11-002.
- Static/offline fallback: static paired diagram.
- Transition to next slide: `এখন কোনদিকে বাঁকবে তা predict করি।`

### L1-S11 — Rarer → Denser: normal-এর দিকে

- Visual/resource: custom ray diagram.
- Visual purpose: Explain
- Teacher note: bind `r < i` to the shown oblique case; no Snell calculations.
- Student action/question: predict the refracted path before reveal.
- Expected response: toward normal.
- Segment-response role: predict
- Estimated time: 2 min
- Source reference(s): NCTB printed pp.110–111 / PDF pp.115–116; S2/S3.
- Static/offline fallback: static diagram.
- Transition to next slide: `উল্টো পথে গেলে কী হবে?`
