## Coverage status vocabulary

- `MAPPED_CORE` — assigned to non-skippable lesson content.
- `MAPPED_CORE/FLEX` — core idea covered; an additional example/activity may be FLEX.
- `MAPPED_PRACTICE` — used mainly for guided practice, hinge, exit, or assessment.
- `MAPPED_AS_RETRIEVAL_SUMMARY` — source summary/terms are represented through recap/retrieval rather than copied as a separate textbook-summary slide.
- `MAPPED_CORE_WITH_SOURCE_CHECK` — planned as core but requires source/science reconciliation before freeze.
- `HOMEWORK` — intentionally assigned outside classroom CORE/FLEX.
- `STRETCH` — appendix/enrichment only.
- `OMITTED_WITH_REASON` — deliberately excluded with a documented reason.
- `SOURCE_ISSUE` — cannot be finalized until the source discrepancy is resolved.
